Tutorial for Spherical Stereo Rectification

The goal of this tutorial is to provide an intuitive geometric explanation of Spherical Stereo Rectification and an introduction to fisheye images and their equirectangular projection. While the underlying concepts have been known for many years, there exist relatively few introductory resources that explain them from a geometric perspective, unlike classical planar stereo, which is covered extensively in textbooks, courses, and tutorials.

The concepts presented here emerged between 2005 and 2007 through several independent contributions to fisheye and spherical stereo vision. During this period, different researchers developed related ideas concerning spherical epipolar geometry, rectification, and dense stereo estimation. Some of the most influential works are:
  • Li: "Real-Time Spherical Stereo"
  • Abraham and Förstner: "Fish-Eye Stereo Calibration and Epipolar Rectification"
  • Arican and Frossard: "Dense Disparity Estimation from Omnidirectional Images"
